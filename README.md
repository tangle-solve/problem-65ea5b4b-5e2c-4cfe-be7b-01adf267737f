### ALGO-000044: Deterministic polynomial-time white-box PIT
- **Subfield:** Algebraic complexity and polynomial identity testing
- **Open:** Given an arithmetic circuit of polynomially bounded degree over a specified effective field, decide deterministically in polynomial time whether it computes the zero polynomial.
- **Status:** Randomized polynomial-time identity testing has been available for decades. General deterministic polynomial-time testing remains open despite many restricted-model algorithms.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Would derandomize a fundamental primitive in symbolic algebra and imply significant circuit lower bounds through known connections.
- **Key works:** Schwartz (1980); Zippel (1979); Kabanets and Impagliazzo (2004), *Derandomizing Polynomial Identity Tests Means Proving Circuit Lower Bounds*; Hu, van Melkebeek, and Morgan (2024), DOI: 10.4086/toc.2024.v020a001.
